# Argus — Next-Generation Human Verification

Argus is an advanced liveness detection and identity verification platform built for the **Google Solution Challenge 2026**. By combining facial biomechanics, heart-rate estimation (rPPG), and blockchain-backed security, Argus ensures that the person on the other side of the screen is a real, living human being.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Multi-Modal Liveness Detection
Argus doesn't just look at a photo. It analyzes:
*   **Facial Biometrics**: Real-time detection using Google ML Kit.
*   **rPPG Signal Extraction**: Detects the tiny color changes in your skin caused by your heartbeat to prove you are living.
*   **Behavioral Intelligence**: Analyzes natural blinking patterns and head movements.

### 2. Intelligent Challenge-Response
To stop "Replay Attacks" (video recordings or deepfakes), Argus issues randomized challenges:
*   **Dynamic Instructions**: "Blink twice" or "Turn your head."
*   **Reaction Timing**: Precision tracking (down to the millisecond) to distinguish between human reflexes and automated bot scripts.

### 3. Google-Native Trust Ledger
Every successful verification is anchored to a **Cryptographic Ledger** powered by Google Cloud.
*   **Immutability**: Each result is mathematically linked to the one before it, creating a chain of trust.
*   **Official Verification**: Results are digitally signed by hardware-secured keys (Google Cloud KMS), making them impossible to forge.

## 📱 Using the App

1.  **Alignment**: Place your face inside the guide and ensure good lighting.
2.  **Signal Collection**: Hold still for 5–10 seconds while the system synchronizes with your heart rate.
3.  **Complete Challenges**: Follow the on-screen instructions (e.g., blink or turn your head).
4.  **Instant Result**: Get a liveness score and a verified "Blockchain" certificate ID.

## 🛡️ Privacy & Security
*   **Privacy First**: Facial analysis is performed locally on your device.
*   **Secure Cloud**: All verification records are stored using enterprise-grade encryption on Google Cloud Platform.
*   **Tamper-Proof**: The blue "Secured by Google Ledger" badge on your result screen is your mathematical proof of authenticity.
